## How this research was done, and its limits

Searches were run on 2026-10-13 from a sandbox whose outbound access to official sites (ndpc.gov.ng, fccpc.gov.ng, gazette and ministry sites, supabase.com) **was blocked**, so **no primary source (statute text, regulation, gazette notice) could be read**. Everything below comes from search-result summaries of law-firm and consultancy articles. Treat each row as a **lead to verify against the official document**, not as a statement of law. Nothing is cited as legally mandatory unless a source says so, and even then it needs confirmation. Counsel should obtain the primary texts listed in the last column.

## Conclusions allowed by this research

1. The NDPA 2023 and the NDPC's GAID 2025 are the data-protection instruments to verify first; the 72-hour breach-reporting figure and the major-importance registration regime are the two items most likely to change operating procedures.
2. The FCCPA is the consumer-protection statute to verify; the marketplace's refund policy must not remove legally protected consumer rights.
3. Counsel must decide the electronic-evidence position of the drawn signature and the rider classification.
4. **No statement in the drafts is claimed to be legally mandatory by this research.**
