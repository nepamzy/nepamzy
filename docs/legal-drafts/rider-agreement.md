## 1. Eligibility and verification

## 2. Transport

Riders may deliver **on foot, by public transport, or by private vehicle**. A car or motorcycle is **not** required. Licences or documents are required **only** for the vehicle actually used and **only** where the law requires it. [COUNSEL: legal requirements by mode.]

## 3. Assignments

A rider may hold **at most five** accepted active deliveries at once (enforced by the system). An order has one active rider. Accepting an offer is the rider's choice; cancellation and failed-delivery treatment [OWNER: undecided].

## 4. Location (GPS)

## 6. Handover and delivery proof

Deliver only to the account holder, who must provide the delivery confirmation (currently a drawn signature through the customer's own session using a one-time token the rider cannot see). The rider must not mark a delivery complete that did not occur; false claims lead to suspension and possible further action. If a customer cannot provide the proof, the rider contacts support and must not complete it themselves.
