## Gaps counsel should decide

- Whether a cookie banner is needed (today only strictly necessary storage is used).
- Whether the company must be registered before the terms can be published, and which name appears in them.
- Whether holding customer money until delivery, and releasing it to vendors later, needs a licensed escrow or another structure. The app does not hold funds in escrow today and says so.
- Whether the signature comparison and the ID card photo need explicit consent or a data-protection impact assessment.
- Whether school-community members (students, military, lecturers) can be treated differently in the terms.
