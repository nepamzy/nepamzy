## 5. Financial-model report

Full detail in `docs/financial-model.md`. Checkout total = items − product discount + delivery; **no platform fee**; the old fee is removed from pricing, stored order totals for new orders, the Paystack amount (taken from `orders.total_kobo`), the receipt and the UI. `orders.platform_fee_kobo` remains as a column and historical orders are untouched (old orders still carry their stored fee: K75). Commission = half-up(base x 450 bps); AFIT = half-up(base x 300 bps); Paystack budget = commission − AFIT. Delivery excluded. Refunds reverse proportionally with cumulative rounding; whole refund reverses exactly everything. Idempotency: `unique(order_id)` on lines, `unique(refund_id)` on adjustments, plus existing refund locks and caps. Authoritative records: the two settlement tables (immutable). Vendor page shows finalised, pending estimate (current rate), refunds, calculated amount due, and "payouts not enabled". **Simulated/absent**: payouts, rider earnings, real Paystack and real refunds. **Needs approval before real money**: funds flow (Paystack and counsel), payout design, provider-fee treatment on refunds, rider payment, tax.
