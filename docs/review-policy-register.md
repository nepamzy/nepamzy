# Review policy: current behaviour and the decisions it waits for

Everything in the "Current behaviour" column is an **engineering default**, chosen so the feature is safe while the owner decides. None of it is an approved policy. Security rules (ownership, eligibility check on the server, moderation permissions, audit) are not up for relaxation and are listed separately at the end.

| #   | Topic                        | Current behaviour (enforced in the database)                                                                                                            | Consequence of changing it                                                                                               | Safe interim choice                                                 | Decides                |
| --- | ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| 1   | Which orders may be reviewed | Only `COMPLETED` or `PARTIALLY_REFUNDED`, by the order's own customer                                                                                   | Allowing fully refunded or disputed orders invites retaliation reviews; excluding partial refunds hides real experiences | Keep as is                                                          | Owner                  |
| 2   | One review per order         | Unique per order; a second attempt is refused                                                                                                           | Allowing several per shop over time needs a rule for repeat customers; allowing several per order enables review-bombing | Keep as is                                                          | Owner                  |
| 3   | Editing                      | Not allowed; reviews are immutable                                                                                                                      | Edits need an edit history, a time limit and re-moderation                                                               | Keep as is (a mistaken review can only be reported/hidden by staff) | Owner                  |
| 4   | Deleting                     | Not allowed for anyone; staff can only hide, administrators can restore                                                                                 | Deletion by the author conflicts with the audit trail and with any retention rule                                        | Keep as is                                                          | Owner + counsel        |

## Rules that stay regardless of the decisions above

The reviewer must be the verified customer of that order; eligibility is checked in the database, not the browser; a shop's own members cannot review their shop; ratings and counts are never accepted from the client; moderation needs a role, a reason and leaves an audit entry; hiding never changes an order, a payment or a refund; moderators cannot decide their own reviews or reports.
